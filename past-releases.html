2006

Montepuluciano

Love Italian varietals? Looking for a great Italian style wine? In California? Look no further! The first vintage of our 2006 Montepuluciano. Montepulciano is an Italian varietal. In 2006, only 50 tons of Montepulciano were produced in the entire state of California. We got a little over a ton! The grapes that we purchased are from the Nova's Dunnigan Hills Vineyard. Our Montepulciano has been made in the classic style. An earthy nose of moist forest floor, spice, and olde English roses. Followed by ripe dark fruits-plum, black cherries, almond, a hint of cedar and bitter chocolate. A smoky plum finish. Firm tannins and balanced acidity make this a great food wine. It is suave and sophisticated. We only made 65 cases. Since it is new for us and so rare, we have been drinking this with friends and colleagues to determine when it will be ready for release. Recently the response has been "WOW!!!! Smooth, delicious, BERRIES. When can we get some?" This wine says "Drink me now!"
